# Session 2: The Marsh and the Pumpakin King

**Date:** October 24, 2026
**Location:** [Rockfall](/Locations/Locations.md#Rockfall), [Swampy Pass](/Locations/Locations.md#Swampy-Pass), [Shrine of Attack-o-Lanterns](/Locations/Locations.md#Shrine-of-Attack-o-Lanterns), [Zanzibahar](/Locations/Locations.md#Zanzibahar)
**Objective:** Escort sick refugees to Zanzibahar via the marshlands.

---

## 📖 Session Summary

### Celebrations and New Commissions

Following the rescue of the missing family, the party returned to [Rockfall](/Locations/Locations.md#Rockfall) to celebrate. [Joren](/NPCs/NPCs.md#Constable-Joren) and [Aja Appleborn](/NPCs/NPCs.md#Aja-Appleborn) greeted them, with Aja tentatively inquiring about other survivors (hoping for news of her husband). During the festivities, [Herb](/NPCs/NPCs.md#Herb) gifted [Sicarious](/Characters/Sicarious.md) an Enzi-shaped pipe ("Yoink!"), and lit up joints with the group—though Herb noticed Sicarious only faked smoking it. [Silvie](/NPCs/NPCs.md#Silvie), a blue dragonborn innkeeper, shooed Herb away and hosted the party at the [Dreaming Dragon Inn](/Locations/Locations.md#The-Dreaming-Dragon-Inn) with vegetable soup and ale while [Blaise](/Characters/Blaise.md) played celebratory music.

The party closely examined the miniature [Kuona](/MinorArcana/MinorArcana.md#Kuona) in private after noticing it drew unwanted attention as a magical creature. It rejected carrots and cream, showed enthusiasm for a sack of dirt, emitted reduced divine magic energy, and seemed to "look" at them despite having no eyes. When touched by Blaise and friendly-roughed up by [Cassius](/Characters/Cassius.md), it "ate" one of each of their spell slots. During the long rest, Sicarious (needing only a 4-hour trance) set up "super effective" booby-traps, and nothing disturbed the camp.

In the morning, an exhausted-looking Joren provided coffee, soup, biscuits, and rations, commissioning the party to transport sick passengers to [Zanzibahar](/Locations/Locations.md#Zanzibahar) via the **Swampy Pass** for a reward upon safe arrival. The party departed using sledges pulled by three lean, skittish [Swamp Stompers](/NPCs/NPCs.md#Swamp-Stompers) whose "floppy flappy feet" allowed them to traverse the marsh:

- **Sledge 1 (Stompy):** An injured woman (Lily) and a baby.
- **Sledge 2 (FluffyBum):** A sick man with a wet cough.
- **Sledge 3 (Magistrate Dagda):** An unconscious man and a dog-like creature.

Before leaving, Aja had an awkward, potentially flirty interaction with Blaise, warned of the marsh's dangers, and presented Cassius with 15 GP and a letter on Rockfall Letterhead declaring him an official delegate of Rockfall. Nearby, a silver dragonborn acolyte from a church in [Noa](/Characters/Noa.md)'s hometown performed a rite; Blaise tried to play along and was hit with _Silence_ before Noa realized it was a funeral ritual. Choosing the quicker, more dangerous marsh path over the heavily traveled main road, the party headed into the wilderness.

### The Treacherous Marsh and Sir Incanus

Traversing the marsh, Blaise flew behind the sledges dodging webs, trees, and dive-bombing frog-like **Cricket Swarms**. At camp, Blaise joked about offering alcohol to the breastfeeding woman in Sledge 1 (_"'I must confess, 'tis soup!"_) before soothing the group with music. While Cassius maintained the carts, the mini-Kuona grew fussy and ate one of Noa's spell slots. During night watch, the crickets abruptly vanished, leaving [Barvin](/Characters/Barvin.md) feeling heavy and empty before a single distant cricket sounded; soon, swarms of intelligent-seeming crickets watched the party from the trees, making Sicarious deeply uneasy. By morning, a cricket harmonized with Blaise in a war-march rhythm, followed by a "horrible lament" perceived differently across the party: Blaise and Sicarious thought it sounded great, Cassius and Noa thought it sounded terrible, and Barvin simply heard a song.

Scouting ahead, Cassius, Blaise, and Noa found the source of the warbling: [Sir Incanus](/NPCs/NPCs.md#Sir-Incanus), a young, ambitious Tabaxi wizard from a poor background with a chip on his shoulder, hanging upside-down in a crude tree trap after being submerged multiple times. Blaise cut the ropes, but Cassius fumbled the catch and both "splatted" on the ground as the trap broke; Incanus cast _prestidigitation_ to clean himself. Incanus blamed the **Bullywugs** (claiming at least six were at a nearby shrine tied to the past), noted that crickets can find anyone if you possess their **pearl**, and offered 200 GP for an escort to the shrine over a day's travel away.

Back at camp, Lily let Barvin hold the baby, and Cassius intentionally "fed" the mini-Kuona a spell slot. That night, the crickets vanished again, and the sounds of something being killed echoed in the distance—which Incanus identified as a Bullywug. By morning, the mini-Kuona had crawled out of its dirt sack to snuggle with Noa, favoring her rock-like skin.

### Battle at the Shrine of Attack-o-Lanterns

Hiding the sledges and passengers in the bushes, the party approached the ruined temple decorated with pumpakins. They found the body of an ornately dressed Bullywug chieftain wearing an unusual necklace and a Warforged headpiece—which a nearby pumpakin suddenly swallowed.

The party was ambushed by **Attack-o-Lanterns (Pumpakins)** and **Giant Spiders**. Early in the skirmish, Giant Spiders grappled and poisoned Sicarious and landed two devastating critical hits on Barvin, knocking him unconscious. Meanwhile, the pumpakin that swallowed the headpiece backed off, vanished, and seemingly cast a spell, summoning the flying, scythe-wielding **Pumpakin King**. Cassius revived Barvin with healing, Barvin recovered to finish off a spider with _Flurry of Blows_, and the party rallied—culminating in Noa slaying the Pumpakin King with a decisive _Starburst_ and a snappy one-liner.

### Ancient Secrets and Visions

After the battle, Bullywugs and crickets approached; the crickets bit and fed on the pumpakins, returning to normal, happy behavior. Examining the ruins, Incanus and Noa noted badly damaged statues surrounding a central winged figure that Noa believed to be **Nephya**, who once helped Enzi. The party recovered ancient severed **Warforged** remains (an arm, leg, torso, and the newer-looking headpiece) covered in pumpakin and Bullywug toothmarks. During a short rest, Blaise scavenged a copper chalice (~25 GP), finger bone-dice (~25 GP), and gemstones (~50 GP). Cassius identified the chieftain's necklace as the Warforged's **Soul Core**; the party assembled the parts and Cassius kissed the core, but it failed to activate. Incanus advised traveling to **Urusha** or seeking **Taranis** to repair it, so the party packed the remains.

Returning to camp for a long rest, the party experienced vivid dreams and nocturnal visitors:

- **Barvin's Nightmare:** Reliving the spider's venomous bite, Barvin fell blindly through a weightless void toward rushing water before unseen hands twisted his arms and slipped a noose around his neck—snapping him back to the waking world.
- **Blaise & Sicarious:** Blaise dreamed of family and the ocean; Sicarious dreamed of an orgy.
- **Sanguine Lenegara:** Fluffy, harmless, insomnia-curing mosquitoes bit Noa, Cassius, and Blaise (1 HP loss each) while trying to drink from their battle wounds. Noa and Cassius shooed them away (Noa threw a rock to scatter them), while Sicarious kept one as a pet.

### Arrival at Zanzibahar

In the morning, the party exited the swamp to the grass, roads, and ocean bay of [Zanzibahar](/Locations/Locations.md#Zanzibahar). A mounted fire Genasi met them on the road to take custody of the refugees. Incanus paid his 200 GP fee, split evenly among the five party members (40 GP each), and the party boarded Stompy, FluffyBum, and Magistrate Dagda at [Ilym's Exotic Animals](/Locations/Locations.md#Ilyms-Exotic-Animals) to rest and heal.

Before departing, the party checked a local message board featuring several leads and notices:

- **Wanted (Black Lady):** Reward for info on the deadly pirate crew; report **IN PERSON** (added in red pen) to [Alun Calder](/NPCs/NPCs.md#Alun-Calder) at the [Constable's Office](/Locations/Locations.md#The-Constables-Office).
- **Wanted (White Mosquito):** 500 GP reward for a rare white mosquito via [Elim](/NPCs/NPCs.md#Elim) of [Elim’s Exotic Ænimals](/Locations/Locations.md#Elims-Exotic-Aenimals).
- **Magistrate's Notice:** Monthly Boasting canceled; written complaints go in the mailbox. Scribbled notes read _"WHERE ARE THE FISH"_, _"Tirta I love you, please return my messages"_, _"THE END IS NIGH, THE GOD KING IS DEAD"_, and _"Diddum was here"_ in red—hiding a message in Thieves' Cant inviting Sicarious to [The Merle Inn](/Locations/Locations.md#The-Merle-Inn) for work.
- **Agent Sebastian:** Flyer featuring a smiling, muscular dragonborn offering to take cases ignored by the Constable at the [Deluxe Dragon Detective Department](/Locations/Locations.md#Deluxe-Dragon-Detective-Department).
