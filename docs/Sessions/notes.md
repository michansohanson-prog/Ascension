VIII. The Road to Zanzibahar In the morning, the party pressed on, leaving the swamp behind for grass, roads, and the ocean bay of Zanzibahar. On the road, a mounted fire Genasi approached, taking the refugees. Incanus provided 200GP, which was split evenly (40GP each) among the main party members. The group boarded their "Swamp Stompers" at Ilym's Exotic Animals to rest and heal.

Upon approaching a message board, several notices caught their attention:

Wanted: Information on the "Black Lady" pirate crew; report in person to Alun Calder at the Constable's Office.
Wanted: A rare white Mosquito (500GP reward) via Elim of Elim’s Exotic Ænimals.
Public Notices: The Magistrate's monthly boasting is canceled; complaints must be written and placed in a mailbox. Existing complaints included: "WHERE ARE THE FISH" in stiff letters; "Tirta I love you..." jotted quickly; "THE END IS NIGH, THE GOD KING IS DEAD" pressed deep into the wood; and "Diddum was here" written in flamboyant red Thieves Cant, inviting people to The Merle Inn.
Legal: Agent Sebastian is available for cases ignored by the Constable, operating from the Deluxe Dragon Detective Department (depicted by a smiling, muscular dragonborn man).
