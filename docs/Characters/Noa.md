## ✨ Personality & Traits

Noa is a wizard of enigmatic tastes and mysterious motives. She maintains a strict, almost defensive secrecy regarding her history and the source of her magical components, at least until session 1. She possesses an air of guarded authority—her most prized possession (and most dangerous secret) is hidden beneath her hat.

## 📜 Lore & History

### I. Origins: The Redacted Realm

Noa's specific home city remains a mystery to all but her closest allies. Her origins are currently REDACTED by the archives of the Order.

### II. Arcane Exploration

Noa is known for her "no questions" policy. She moves through the world as an independent agent of magic, whose blue-tinted arcane aura often serves as a warning to those who dare approach her secrets too closely.
