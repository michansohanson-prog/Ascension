## ✨ Personality & Traits

Sicarious is the shadow in the room—the person who was never there until they decided to be. He operates with a level of professional stealth that borders on the supernatural, viewing infiltration not just as a skill, but as an art form. He is playful but dangerous; he may leave you with a prank one moment and open a door you didn't know existed the next.
