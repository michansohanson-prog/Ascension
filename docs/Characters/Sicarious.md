## ✨ Personality & Traits

Sicarious is the shadow in the room—the person who was never there until they decided to be. He operates with a level of professional stealth that borders on the supernatural, viewing infiltration not just as a skill, but as an art form. He is playful but dangerous; he may leave you with a prank one moment and open a door you didn't know existed the next.

## 📜 Lore & History

### I. Origins: [REDACTED]

Sicarious’s past is shrouded in shadows. His history is currently unavailable to common records, known only as a series of movements through high-security zones and silent streets.

### II. The Art of the Unseen

Sicarious specializes in being "in places he isn't supposed to be." He thrives on the invisible threads of organized crime and clandestine operations, making sure that by the time anyone realizes he was there, he is already gone.
