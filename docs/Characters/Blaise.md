## ✨ Personality & Traits

Blaise is a "Skyward Swashbuckler" who traded the high seas for high notes. As an Aarakocra with keen hawk-like instincts, he balances maritime pirate life with a flair for performance. He has a reputation for being sharp—both in his observation of others and his instinct for identifying anything shiny. While his friendly chirps might suggest a peaceful nature, he possesses the predatory instincts of a bird of prey.

## 📜 Lore & History

### I. Origins: The Port of Zanzibahar

Blaise hails from Zanzibahar, the port city devoted to the Goddess Bahare. Growing up in a cultural melting pot defined by large marinas and maritime trade, Blaise learned early on that the waves of the ocean were just as complex as the social waters of the docks.

### II. The Pirate’s Path

Trading his traditional seafaring life for musical performance, Blaise became a pirate of a different sort. He navigates trade routes looking for "scores"—which may be literal treasure or simply a really good snack. His maritime instincts are sharp; he views shiny objects as public property by default.
