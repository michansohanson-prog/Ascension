# Ascendency

## What we know

### Nature and Status

- **Ascendant Status:** "Ascendant" is a title or status held by individuals, such as Petra Helm, who has held her Ascendancy for 346 years.
- **Connection to the Land:** An Ascendant appears to have a profound physical and magical connection to the environment. For example, Petra Helm's body could shift from sand into red dragon scales, and she was described as being "sand, stone, and earth." When she moved through the streets, there was often no evidence that she had been there at all.
- **Connection to Enzi:** The gifts of the God King Enzi helped Petra move quickly through her kingdom, suggesting that Ascendancy is tied to his influence or favor.

### Physical and Magical Manifestations

- **Resilience:** Even as an Ascendant, individuals can still experience physical pain from significant injuries (e.g., a burn from a past fire).
- **Unraveling into Magic:** In extreme circumstances, an Ascendant's body can undergo a violent transformation. The text describes an Ascendant's flesh coming undone in "spools of golden light," forming threads that weave together into a cord before eventually "unfurling back into pure magic."

### Cultural and Artistic Context

- **Enzi’s Ascension:** There are "carved motifs of Enzi’s ascension" that decorate the stones of ornate pathways in the kingdom of Enziret, indicating that the concept of "ascension" is a significant religious or historical theme in the world.
- **Unresolved Mystery:** While its effects are observed, its exact mechanics and nature remain unknown to the players/characters.
