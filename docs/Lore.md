# 🌍 Welcome to the World

Welcome! This is your primary resource for exploring the history, geography, and people of our world. Use the sidebar to navigate through detailed guides on specific regions, deific entities, and historical archives.

---

## The Divine Order

On this continent, gods have always existed with the people as visible elemental powers that occasionally roam. **The gods are dragons** (mixed with other animals). Because of this, dragonborns and genasi/elemental races are far more common than in other places.

## God-Touched Magic

People are frequently 'god-touched' by the magic around them, often possessing an extra element of power. For example, a person might have flame abilities instead of standard racial traits.

## 🗺️ The Continent at a Glance

Explore the major regions in detail via their respective pages:

- **Enziret:** The Fallen Kingdom (Currently in state of emergency).
- **Zanzibahar:** A vibrant cultural melting pot and primary port city.
- **Urusha:** A hub of technological innovation and high-energy revelry.
- **Mayua:** The agricultural heartland and breadbasket of the continent.
- **Nyalima:** High mountain temples and desolate religious training grounds.

## ⚠️ Current State of Affairs

::: danger **Status: Emergency in Enziret**
:::
The world is currently reeling from the **Cataclysm at Enziret**. Following a massive earthquake, the God King's mountain erupted with lava and toxic gas. The kingdom has been devastated, the God King remains missing, and the refugee crisis is beginning to reshape the continent’s social landscape.
