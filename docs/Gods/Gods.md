# Gods

This page contains information regarding the Gods of the world.

## Enzi

### The God King

- **Domain** Enziret
- **Status:** Missing / Unknown Location
- **Description:** A gargantuan capybara dragon whose magic radiates a peaceful and calming aura throughout the world. He resides atop a massive mountain where he watches over his vast kingdom, Enziret.
  ::: danger Very recently, Enzi's mountain erupted in the middle of the night, spewing toxic gasses into the sky. Lava poured down the mountainside and the earth quaked, devastating the majority of his kingdom. Enzi's mountain is currently inaccessible and his presence can no longer be felt, leaving his people in a state of panic.
  :::
