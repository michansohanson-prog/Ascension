# Gods

This page contains information regarding the Gods of the world.

## Enzi

### The God King, The Golden Dreamer

- **Domain** Enziret
- **Associations** All forms of earth and rock, loyalty, strength, peace, dreams, and immutability.
- **Status:** Missing / Unknown Location
- **Description:** The savior of Hadara. He defeated the other Gods and was able to unite the Major Arcana in peace. Since then, he lies in slumber atop Mount Enziret; as his kingdom has grown around his resting place, he continues to radiate a constant Aura of Tranquility.
  ::: danger Very recently, Enzi's mountain erupted in the middle of the night, spewing toxic gases into the sky. Lava poured down the mountainside and the earth quaked, devastating the majority of his kingdom. Enzi's mountain is currently inaccessible and his presence can no longer be felt, leaving his people in a state of panic.
  :::

## Bahare

### Benevolenta, The Sea Witch

- **Domain** Zanzibahar
- **Associations** The oceans and tides, adaptability, constitution, grace, fickleness, and luck.
- **Description:** A behemoth of the deep and a lucky charm for all sailors, Bahare is known for turning the tides of fate in favor of those who worship her. To see her silhouette beneath the waves is to know your voyage will be successful, as she guides fishermen to plentiful waters, and has been known to pacify storms.
  ::: warning If another ship arrives carrying a crew who she favors more, she has been known to leave a crew for another.
  :::

## Mayren

### Our Mournful and Merciful Lady, The Shrouded Vision

- **Domain** Mayua
- **Associations** Flora and fauna, empathy, growth, wisdom, beauty, love, and sorrow.
- **Description:** Rarely seen, Mayren shields herself behind an ever-growing expanse of vines and flowers. These growths protect her from the overwhelming emotions of those who intrude upon her territory. While she attempts to block out the world, she still feels their shared pain from a great distance; when she weeps, her tears feed the many rivers that sustain the farmlands of Mayua.

## Nyota

### Nightheritor

- **Domain** Nyalima
- **Associations** Magic, intelligence, ambition, leadership, faith, and egocentrism.
- **Description:** The easiest to spot of the Gods, and yet the most distant of the Major Arcana. Every night, Nyota makes her flight across the heavens, her scales resplendent with Starfall and Seraph. However, there is a cold distance to her, as though she only distantly belongs to Hadara at all. Nevertheless, her devotees are some of the most fanatically committed.

## Hurun & Nurun

### Urun, The Twins, Thunder & Lightning

- **Domain** Urusha
- **Associations** Wind and lightning/thunderstorms, dexterity, freedom, dancing, joy, carelessness.
- **Description:** Torn asunder during the battle with Enzi, the Twin Gods remain eternally linked, their separation manifesting as the very storms that dance across Urusha. They spend most of their time in the atmosphere above Urusha, generating massive electrical storms that power the city, though they can also be seen running delightedly through the deserts and plains.
  ::: info Known by some to have wondrous healing powers. Little else is known at this time.
