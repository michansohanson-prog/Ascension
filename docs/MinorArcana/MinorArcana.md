# Minor Arcana

This page documents the Minor Arcana we discover on our journey.

## Kuona

![Kuona](/MinorArcana/Kuona.webp)

A lesser god residing underneath Mount Enziret.

Seemingly driven mad by some unknown source, Kuona was defeated in battle. Upon defeat, the lesser god seemed to disintegrate. Within the sand-like substance was this miniature version which is now in the care of the Wizard Noa.
