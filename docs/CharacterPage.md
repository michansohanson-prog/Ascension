# Characters

## Blaise de La Mer

_Aarakocra Bard_

::: info
**Origin:** A very high-up tree. Probably.
Blaise is an avian musician who specializes in making bird noises that people occasionally describe as "music."
:::

::: tip
_Pro-tip:_ Don't ask him to hit the high notes unless you want your ears to ring for at least twenty minutes.
:::

::: warning
**Caution:** He might try to perch on your shoulder. This is not a request; it is a bird thing.
:::

::: danger
**Danger:** His "bardic inspiration" is mostly just him screaming very loudly in your general direction.
:::

::: details
**Background Lore:**
How the hell should I know?
:::

## Noa

_Wizard_

::: info
**Origin:** A library that was possibly haunted by a very grumpy ghost.
Noa spends her time yelling at reality until it does what she wants, usually in exchange for snacks or silence.
:::

::: tip
_Pro-tip:_ If the air starts glowing blue, it’s probably a good time to take a step back and reconsider your life choices.
:::

::: warning
**Caution:** She has a strict "no questions" policy regarding where she gets her magical components from.
:::

::: danger
**Danger:** Do not touch her hat. It is currently holding together several very fragile secrets.
:::

::: details
**Background Lore:**
I honestly have no idea.
:::

## Sicarious

_Rogue_

::: info
**Origin:** A dark alleyway that was way too clean to be safe.
Sicarious is exceptionally good at being in places he isn't supposed to be, and making sure nobody notices him while he does it.
:::

::: tip
_Pro-tip:_ If you see him, he has already seen you. It’s not a competition; he just won.
:::

::: warning
**Caution:** Do not stand directly behind him. It is considered an insult to his profession and may result in a very unnecessary prank.
:::

::: danger
**Danger:** He can open doors. You really don't want to know which ones, or why he’s opening them.
:::

::: details
**Background Lore:**
Some secrets are better left unasked.
:::

## Cassius

_Cleric_

::: info
**Origin:** A very intense prayer circle in a very drafty chapel.
Cassius talks to the "Big Guy" and expects things to happen. He is mostly here to make sure everyone stays on the right side of the cosmic balance.
:::

::: tip
_Pro-tip:_ Try not to make him angry; his deity has a very specific set of grievances that he'd love to share with you.
:::

::: warning
**Caution:** His moral judgment is... intense. Be prepared for an unscheduled sermon at any moment.
:::

::: danger
**Danger:** Do not argue with his faith. You will lose, and the public embarrassment will be entirely your responsibility.
:::

::: details
**Background Lore:**
The divine mystery remains... mysterious.
:::

## Barvin

_Thunder-infused Open-Hand Monk_

::: info
**Origin:** A mountain peak where lightning strikes once a week just to be "edgy."
Barvin hits things very hard and makes a lot of loud noises while doing it. It's simple, effective, and very noisy.
:::

::: tip
_Pro-tip:_ If you hear thunder but the sky is clear, Barvin is probably nearby. Prepare accordingly.
:::

::: warning
**Caution:** His "open hand" approach is less about being peaceful and more about closing his fist really, really fast.
:::

::: danger
**Danger:** Do not get caught in his "thunderous" radius. It’s very loud for everyone involved, especially the neighbors.
:::

::: details
**Background Lore:**
Why would I tell you? You'd just want to be hit by the thunder anyway.
:::
